Solo project · GameLab 3 · Custom engine · C++20 & Vulkan

Voxel Survivors

A first-person wave-survival shooter running on an engine I wrote myself. The terrain is fully destructible in real time.

  • Engine from scratch: C++20 with Vulkan compute raytracing — a DAG for static geometry plus sparse voxel octrees per chunk.
  • Three schools of magic: fire, lightning and earth, each with two alternative level-4 abilities (e.g. Meteor Storm vs. Firestorm).
  • Destruction: explosions, beams and rising earth spikes carve away terrain in real time.
  • My part: engine, gameplay systems and documentation (handbook, Doxygen API docs).

Solo project · GameLab 1 · Unity 2022.3 LTS · C#

Chromaflux

A 2D action platformer about a chameleon that switches color to slip past projectiles and unlock four different abilities.

  • Core mechanic: a color wheel switches between green, blue, red and yellow — projectiles matching your current color pass through harmlessly.
  • Abilities: dash (green), grappling hook (blue), ranged projectile (red), pogo attack (yellow).
  • Two boss fights: a turtle firing color-coded bullet rings, and a spider combining ground charges with lingering webs.
  • My part: built entirely on my own — code, design, level building and art direction.

Gravity Gauntlet

A sci-fi puzzle platformer built around gravity manipulation, created in the university course “Game Studio”. Part of the team is taking it further as Fair Enough Games — coming to Steam.

Team project (2 people) · University course “Game Lab II” · VR multiplayer

CreateWithVR — Prisoner's Dilemma

A VR multiplayer social experiment that turns the prisoner's dilemma into a physical game at a shared virtual table, with hand tracking and networked state.

  • Networking: Netcode for GameObjects + Unity Relay for peer-to-peer sessions between two VR headsets.
  • VR interaction: XR Interaction Toolkit, OpenXR and Oculus hand tracking instead of controller buttons.
  • Custom framework: a reusable “GT” framework for table-based interactions — buttons, boards and scoreboard.
  • My part: scoreboard, table and board setup, button prefabs, NPC head models. Networking logic and the core rules came from my teammate.
